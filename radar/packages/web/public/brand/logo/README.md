# Live Collab logo — "Hub"

Six directions around one shared room. Five bars are contributors (people and their IBM Bob agents); one bar is replaced by a dot, the person who is live right now. The empty centre is the room where everyone's state meets (the Durable Object room). When animated, the dot can move between positions.

Black and white only. Wordmark: IBM Plex Sans, `live` 600 + `collab` 400, lowercase, letter-spacing -0.03em.

| File | Use |
|---|---|

Rules: keep clear space of at least one bar width around the mark; do not rotate, recolour, or add IBM's logo next to it (community project, not an official IBM product).
